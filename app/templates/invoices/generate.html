About Bill Invoices

This generates a shared bills invoice to send to a tenant as a PDF. It covers their pro-rata share of gas, electricity, council tax, and any one-off charges.

Rent is not included here. Rent charges are managed automatically by the system via the rent roll and do not need to be invoiced separately — tenants typically pay by standing order. Use the Rent Roll to track rent balances.
